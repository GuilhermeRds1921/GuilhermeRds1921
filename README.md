# Hello, I'm Guilherme Rodrigues 👋

I'm a **Computer Engineering student at UTFPR – Pato Branco Campus**, with interests in **embedded systems, IoT, software development, applied artificial intelligence, automation, and information security**.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **software and hardware**, developing solutions involving sensors, telemetry, dashboards, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Linux, C/C++, Python, and data integration.

Currently, I am part of the **Neonatal Biometrics Research Group at UTFPR-PB** and I am developing my undergraduate thesis in the field of **machine learning applied to embedded platforms**, evaluating the performance of neural network models on devices such as the Raspberry Pi.
